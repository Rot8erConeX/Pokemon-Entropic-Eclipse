Clover Charm - wild mons have more items
Oval Charm - more likely to find eggs
Shiny Charm - more likely to find shinies
Catching Charm - more likely to get critical catches
Exp. Charm - more exp from battles
Effort Charm - more flexibility for EV training
Balance Charm - makes all encounters (except Island Scan) equally likely
>Type< Charm - if an encounter of >type< exists, makes it so only encounters of >type< will spawn (this will be one unlock and then afterwards, each type's charm will come alongside the corresponding type's Z-Crystal)

Gold Charm - increases gold from Trainer battles
